MANUFACTURING WORKFLOW

6-Step Closed Manufacturing Process Under BRCGS Standards

VINAXO's facility operates under strict critical control points, completely eliminating refined sugars and synthetic bleaching agents to preserve authentic colors, fiber, and natural vitamins.

01

Orchard Sourcing & Inbound Quality Inspection

Harvested from contracted grower cooperatives in Dong Thap, Tien Giang, and Vinh Long. Measuring natural Brix sugar levels, sensory ripeness checks, and pesticide residue screening before warehouse entry.

02

Preparation, Peeling & Ozone Disinfection Wash

Multi-stage washing combined with ozone aeration for outer surface disinfection. Cleanroom-attired workers peel, deseed, and uniformly slice fruit.

03

Low-Temperature Pretreatment & Natural Conditioning

Gentle thermal conditioning inactivates color-changing enzymes naturally, without sulfur dioxide (SO2) bleaching or artificial refined sugars.

04

Low-Heat Dehydration (~60°C) & Vacuum Frying

Circulating convective low-temperature dehydrators gradually remove moisture, preserving pliable texture, dietary fiber, and vitamin C. Vacuum frying is applied for crunchy lines.

05

Centrifugal De-Oiling & Cleanroom Cooling

Vacuum-fried items enter high-capacity centrifuges to extract excess oil, ensuring light crispness and minimal fat content meeting healthy snack standards.

06

Metal Detection, Cleanroom Packaging & Batch Coding

100% of finished lots pass through automated metal detectors. Sealed in multi-layer barrier pouches with nitrogen flushing or export cartons with full traceability barcodes.
